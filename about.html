Beyond the Bricks

I love exploring new trails, especially ones with challenging climbs and rewarding views. Some of my favorite hikes have been Yosemite’s Mist Trail, Mount Tammany in New Jersey, and trails around Mount Rainier. I’m always looking for my next adventure.

Pickleball is one of my favorite ways to stay active, compete, and spend time with friends. I love how a casual game can turn into a surprisingly intense match.

I’ve loved building with LEGO for years, from following detailed sets to experimenting with my own creations. That interest even inspired BrickMatch, a computer vision project I’m developing to identify loose LEGO pieces and recommend things you can build with them.
