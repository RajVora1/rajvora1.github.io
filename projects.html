BrickMatch

Python, PyTorch, YOLO, ResNet-18

Photograph a pile of LEGO and BrickMatch tells you what’s in it, and what you can build with it.

  1. Detect

    YOLO finds each piece in the pile: 90% recall.

  2. Classify

    ResNet-18 names the piece and its color, across 8 colors: 85% accuracy.

  3. Count

    Every detection becomes an inventory, by piece type.

  4. Recommend

    Ranks 10+ builds by how much of each your pile covers, suggests compatible swaps, and lists what’s missing.

I built and labeled the dataset myself, across different lighting and crowding, and split it by physical pile so no pile shows up in both training and test.

ARGOS

Research at George Mason University, 2025

An Intelligent Tutoring System that works out what a student understands and gives real-time, personalized guidance. My part was measuring whether it works.

Does it help students?

  1. Pre-assessment
  2. Tutoring with ARGOS
  3. Post-assessment
  4. +65% average math performance, 50+ students

Is it a good tutor?

  1. Tutor responses in 8 scenarios
  2. Scored on my 30-point rubric
  3. Compared with 4 human evaluators
  4. r = 0.837 agreement with humans

The rubric scores tutoring quality, factual accuracy, instructional adherence and mastery-score reliability.

Presented with 3 peers at the 2025 MIT Undergraduate Research Technology Conference.
